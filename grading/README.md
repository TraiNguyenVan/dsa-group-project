# Grading — Part F Evidence Pack

> The marks your group awards feed into your classmates' attendance & participation score (10% of their final grade). Treat this with the seriousness that implies. **Marks awarded without evidence are void.**

## Workflow

1. **Publish the scheme first** — points per exercise and per criterion, before anyone submits.
2. **Mark independently** — at least two group members mark every submission separately.
3. **Reconcile** — if the two marks differ by > 20%, discuss and record the agreed mark.
4. **Write feedback** — 2–3 sentences per submission: what was right, what was wrong, what to fix.
5. **Submit the evidence pack** — marks, feedback, artefacts — to the lecturer.
6. **Face the appeal** — any student may contest a mark; justify it with the evidence.

## Evidence pack contents (every mark must be traceable)

- [ ] Submitted file or repository link, **with timestamp**
- [ ] Marking sheet: one row per student, one column per criterion
- [ ] Screenshot or terminal log of the submitted code **compiling and running**
- [ ] The written feedback you sent back
- [ ] Names of the two markers + the reconciled mark where they disagreed
- [ ] Attendance record for the session your exercises were set in

## Marking sheet template

`grading/marking_sheet_template.csv` — required columns:

```
student_id | name | ex1 | ex2 | ex3 | ex4 | ex5 | total | marker_A | marker_B | agreed | note
```

Example row: `B21DCCN045 | Tran B | 1 | 2.5 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 8.5 | Hoa | Minh | 8.5 | "ex2: merge step loses duplicates, -0.5"`
